Privacy policy

ClaudeDesktopExports, a Windows app. Last updated: October 2026.

In short

The app runs on your PC. Its developers receive no data from it: there is no server, no account with them, no analytics and no telemetry.

What the app reads on your PC

The conversation files Claude Desktop keeps on the PC, and its own settings. It writes the exported conversations to a folder you choose on the PC.

Google user data

Only if you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • drive.file: to create, update and read the files and folders the app itself makes in your Google Drive (the copy of your exported conversations). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • openid and email: your Google account's address, shown in the app so you know which account is connected.

How it is used: only to keep that copy of your conversations in your own Drive and to bring it to your other PCs. The app never deletes files in your Drive.

Where it is kept: the sign-in (a refresh token) is stored on your PC, encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI). Your Drive files go directly between your PC and Google. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is not used for advertising, and no person reads it.

Other connections

When you ask the app to check for updates, it contacts GitHub to read its release list. Nothing about you or your conversations is sent.

Your choices

  • Disconnect in the app (Settings, Google Drive, Disconnect): the sign-in is removed from the PC.
  • Remove the app's access at any time in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Files the app made in your Drive stay yours; delete them in Drive if you wish.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue on this site's repository.
