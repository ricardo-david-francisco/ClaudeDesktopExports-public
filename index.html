ClaudeDesktopExports

A Windows tray app that saves Claude Desktop conversations as Markdown and HTML pages on your own PC.

What it does

  • Reads the conversation files that Claude Desktop already keeps on the PC and writes each conversation as a Markdown file and an HTML page, in a folder you choose.
  • Searches them, tags them and lets each Windows user of the PC see only what its role allows.
  • Optionally keeps a copy of that folder in your own Google Drive, so your other PCs get it too.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, the app signs in through your browser with Google's own sign-in page and asks only for:

  • drive.file: the files and folders the app itself creates in your Drive. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.
  • openid and email: the address of the Google account, to show which account is connected.

The sign-in stays on your PC, encrypted for your Windows user. The app never deletes anything in your Drive. See the privacy policy.

Runs on your PC

There is no server: the app talks only to Google Drive (when you connect it) and to GitHub (when you check for updates). Nothing is sent to its developers.
